LearnGram Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

The short version

LearnGram has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in the app — your answers to the questionnaire, likes, saved facts and learning progress — is stored only on your own device.

Data stored on your device

  • Your optional first name, chosen interests, daily goal and feed settings.
  • Facts you have seen, liked, saved or hidden, and your review schedule and quiz results.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose Download backup, which saves a file that only you control. You can erase everything at any time under Profile → Reset everything, or by uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data.

Live content

When Live content is switched on (Profile → Settings), the app downloads extra articles and questions directly from:

  • Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) — article titles related to facts you liked are sent as search terms.
  • Open Trivia DB — a category number is requested.

These requests go straight from your device to those services. Like any website, they can see your IP address. LearnGram sends them no name, identifier or personal data. Switch Live content off to stop these requests entirely; the built-in facts work fully offline.

Children

LearnGram collects no personal data, so it is suitable for all ages. Live content comes from Wikipedia and Open Trivia DB and is not moderated by us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
