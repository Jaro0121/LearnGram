# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — first release

- Swipe feed with like, double-tap like, save, share and "show less"
- Five-step onboarding questionnaire (name, interests, daily goal, recall intensity, exploration)
- 200 facts in 10 categories, each with a recall question
- Like-based recommender with explanations ("Because you like #planets") and live re-ranking
- FSRS-inspired spaced-repetition scheduler; recall cards in the feed and a Review tab
- Live content from Wikipedia ("more like this") and Open Trivia DB, with rate-limit back-off and retries
- Explore by category, streaks, XP, daily goal, memory stats
- Backup download / restore, reset
- Offline support and auto-update (service worker), installable PWA with maskable icon and screenshots
- Android app (Capacitor): share sheet, in-app browser, haptics, hardware back button, edge-to-edge safe areas
- Privacy policy (no accounts, no tracking)
